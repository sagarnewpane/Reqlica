How data generation works
Your provider receives your description, table definitions, and published models and handlers—not database rows or runtime secret values. Simple related records are linked to actual parent IDs. Data requiring password hashes, encryption, custom validators, or unsupported storage formats is refused. Generated data stays in the preview until you download it or explicitly confirm an insert. Inserts append to existing ordinary SQLite tables, never create tables or replace records. Tables with triggers/virtual tables are not supported.
